# Limitations (read before quoting a number)

- **Revenue, not profit.** The cost of producing the books is not in the data. A Profit page exists and fills in when a cost file is added (data/costs/).
- **Returns and refunds** written on a few papers are not recorded, so revenue is slightly overstated.
- **Only photographed papers.** Some invoice numbers are missing in every year (other books, or papers never photographed).
- **Handwriting is read, not exported.** Faded or overwritten values were settled by rules and by the owner; a few remain uncertain (the day of a date, a customer's name).
- **16 invoices** have lines that do not add up to the gross written on the paper; they are kept as written.
- **Nominal naira.** Prices rose about 2.8 times from 2019 to 2026; compare copies sold as well as revenue.
- **Partial years.** 2019 starts on 15 January; 2026 stops on 30 September.
- **Customer type** is inferred from the name; a few customers have low-confidence types.
- Anonymised customers cannot be named or contacted from this dataset: that is on purpose.
