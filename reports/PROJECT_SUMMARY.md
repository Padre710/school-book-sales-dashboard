# Project summary (one page)

**What:** 606 handwritten invoices of a Lagos children's book publisher (2019-2026), photographed, read by AI, cross-checked by independent readers, cleaned by 32 written rules and a final review, then analysed in an interactive dashboard.

**Why it is trustworthy:** a value is accepted only when an independent reading of the photo agrees; arithmetic is checked on every invoice; every decision is logged; the owner was asked about 9.7% of the invoices (against 71% when one year was done fully by hand); every dashboard total reconciles to the naira.

**What it shows:** September and August are 55% of revenue; invoices fell from 122 to 34 a year while the average invoice grew from ₦13,272 to ₦46,722; prices rose about 2.8 times; schools are 76% of revenue; the ten biggest customers are 27%.

**What it cannot show:** profit (no cost data yet), unpaid debts, returns.

**Skills shown:** data cleaning at scale with human-in-the-loop review, validation and reconciliation, anonymisation, KPI design, a dashboard with no server (one HTML file), documentation and reproducibility.
